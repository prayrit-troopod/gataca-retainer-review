01 · Executive Summary

Delivery is running at 2.6× the 16-hour allotment, and rising

GATACA is allotted 16 development hours a month. From April to September we averaged 42.02 hours a month, and no month came in at or under 16. The load is rising: April to June averaged 33.35 hours a month, July to September averaged 50.68. This document sets out the monthly numbers, where the hours went, and a 50-hour retainer sized to the work as it now stands.

Consistency, not a one-off
0 of 6 months landed inside 16 hours

The direction of travel
The last quarter ran 52% heavier
April to June totalled 100.06 hours. July to September totalled 152.05. Hours rose four months running, from 19.87 in May to 63.10 in August, and September held at 55.72. The last quarter averaged 50.68 hours a month, 3.17× the allotment.
What the hours went into
Six workstreams, led by PDP work
PDP build and optimisation alone took 64.77 hours, four months of allotted time. Landing pages, the Email Game, new product pages, the NMN sell-off and testing make up the rest. Section 04 breaks them down.
Our recommendation
Move to a 50-hour monthly retainer
50 hours a month at ₹1,200 an hour, ₹60,000 a month, with unused hours carried forward within each quarter. It matches the July to September run-rate and funds a real A/B testing programme, which a 16-hour month cannot fit. Section 07 sets out the plan.

With quarterly rollover, April's 5.59 hours over 50 would have been absorbed by May and June. July to September came to 152.05 hours against 150, so the 50-hour plan matches the latest quarter almost exactly.
Why 50 hours
Reason 01 · The work is growing every month
From 33.35h a month to 50.68h in one quarter
April to June averaged 33.35 hours a month. July to September averaged 50.68, and October used its full 16 hours in its first days. The question isn't whether 16 hours is enough. It is how far above it to set the allotment. 50 hours sits between the six-month average of 42.02 and the latest quarter's 50.68, so it reflects the current workload, not a padded estimate.
Reason 02 · It makes testing possible
One real test took 19.15 hours
The pack-naming test on Biosolve Berberine took more than a full month of today's allotment. The June FBT test was logged at 17 seconds. At 16 hours, every test displaces a build. At 50, testing and conversion-rate consulting get their own room each month.
Reason 03 · Rollover fits launch-driven work
Light months fund heavy ones
Launches arrive in lumps: 16 hours for Iron Bisglycinate, 16 more for DEAR DELHI and Super Loop. Quarterly rollover lets a quiet month cover a launch month. In April to June that would have left 49.94 hours in hand.
Reason 04 · The same rate as today
₹1,200 an hour, unchanged
Nothing in the rate changes. Today, ₹1,20,000 billed across six months covered 252.11 hours, about ₹476 an hour in practice. The 50-hour plan brings the fee back in line with the work.
Reason 05 · A predictable line item
₹60,000 a month, approved once
One fixed monthly figure, a month-end hour report, and an approval step only for hours beyond 50. No more catching up on months of unbilled overage.
